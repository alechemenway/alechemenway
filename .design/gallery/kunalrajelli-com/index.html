06 Correction Plan

What holds the grade back, and what to keep.

  • Deduplicate CSS. 823 duplicate declarations — the largest deduction. Consolidate repeated Tailwind transition groups and utility repeats.
  • Add weight contrast to the type. Typography 80/100: ten sizes, and Playfair runs almost entirely at 400. A 700 display weight would sharpen hierarchy; round 57.6/67.2px to whole steps.
  • Tighten the accent edges. Color 92/100: the warm core is disciplined, but rust, dusty blue, and plum appear once or twice each. Promote them to named tokens or drop them.
  • Keep the craft material. Soft warm shadows + hard 8px offset "stickers" on a blueprint grid. Shadow 90, radius 100, spacing 100.
  • Keep the accessibility. 100/100 — espresso on amber at 11.15:1 (AAA).
