06 Correction Plan

What holds the grade back, and what to keep.

  • Deduplicate CSS. 370 duplicate declarations — the only real deduction (CSS tokenization 75/100). Much of it is the scroll-wave: dozens of near-identical transition groups differing only by a stepped delay (0.15s → 1.53s). Generate those delays from one variable instead of hand-writing each rule.
  • Name the type steps. Typography 90/100: nine sizes including a sub-pixel 13.3333px button size. Round to a small named scale (e.g. 30 / 28 / 27 / 20 / 18 / 17 / 16 / 15 / 13) so the ladder is explicit.
  • Collapse the radius cluster. Border-radius 90/100: seven distinct values (16, 24, 33, 36, 50, 56, 999). The 24px card and 999px pill carry nearly all usage — fold 33 / 36 / 50 / 56 into two named pill tokens.
  • Keep the color discipline. 100/100. A near-monochrome ink-on-white system with the default-blue link as the single accent — ink on white measures 18.73:1 (AAA), white on black 21:1.
  • Keep the flat material. Spacing, shadow, and accessibility all 100/100. No gradients, no backdrop blur, soft single-elevation shadows, and disciplined 2px-based spacing.
