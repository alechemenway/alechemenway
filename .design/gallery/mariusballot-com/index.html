06 Correction Plan

What holds the grade back, and what to keep. The only deduction is CSS tokenization (50/100); everything visual scores 100.

  • Purge unused CSS. 70% of the stylesheet is unused. Tree-shaking the dead rules is the single biggest lever on the 50/100 tokenization score.
  • Deduplicate declarations. 152 duplicate CSS declarations detected. Consolidating them raises tokenization and trims payload — no visual change.
  • Keep the single-typeface discipline. Color, typography, spacing, shadow, and border-radius all score 100/100. IBM Plex Sans at 400 / 500 / 700 carries the entire site on a 4px grid.
  • Keep the flat, accessible material. One elevation token (rgba(0,0,0,0.4) 5px 5px 15px), one 45° teal gradient, white on #191a1a at 17.44:1 (AAA). Accessibility 100/100, though scored on a small 2-pair sample given the canvas-heavy DOM.
