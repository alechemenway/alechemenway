06 Correction Plan

What holds the grade back, and what to keep.

  • Deduplicate CSS. 700 duplicate declarations detected — the only real deduction. Consolidate repeated Tailwind transition groups / utility repeats to lift tokenization headroom.
  • Tighten the type scale. Typography scored 80/100: fifteen distinct sizes including sub-pixel values (94.72, 69.12, 17.28). Round to a named scale (this gallery's display-xl…micro) to raise consistency.
  • Keep the color discipline. 100/100. Ten-color system, gold-on-ink at 10.12:1 (AAA).
  • Keep the flat material. No shadows, hairline borders, gradient depth. Border-radius and spacing both 100/100.
