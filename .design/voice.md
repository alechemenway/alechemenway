# Copy Voice — alechemenway.com

Measured signals plus the patterns visible in the live copy.

## Signals

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Tone | Neutral, declarative |
| Pronoun | First person ("I") for claims, second person ("you") for the reader |
| Heading case | Title Case, balanced length |
| Headings on page | 13 |
| Buttons with copy | 0 (link-led) |

## Patterns

- **Short declarative headings.** Statements, not questions. Confidence without hype.
- **Manual line breaks in display headings** for rhythm and shape, e.g. the hero breaks across three lines.
- **One em dash max**, used for a hard pivot inside a sentence. Otherwise periods do the work.
- **Proof over adjectives.** Copy points to shipped systems and named numbers, not superlatives.
- **First-person ownership** on capability claims ("I sell", "I've shipped", "I've sold").
- **Sentence-case body, Title Case headings.**

## Sample headings

> I sell enterprise software — and build the AI that sells it.

> Where I've sold.

> AI systems I've shipped.

> Deal-Intelligence + Rep Coaching

> Apollo prospecting skill

> Let's talk about the next seat.

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Lead with the claim, back it with a number or a shipped artifact.
- Keep headings to one line of meaning, even when they wrap across lines visually.
- Stay in active voice.

**Don't**
- No hype adjectives (game-changing, best-in-class, revolutionary).
- No filler openers or salesy connective tissue.
- No more than one em dash in a single line of copy.
